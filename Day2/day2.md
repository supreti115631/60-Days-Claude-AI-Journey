## Basics of Prompt Engineering

> *" Prompts is not all about giving gudiding instructions to the Model , its about how effectively
    and efficiently you engage and communicate with your AI"*
                      

**Prompt Engineering** - It's is the process/practice of Desgning and refining structured and clean instructions to guide your AI model
                         towards generating high quality, coherent and relevant responses.

**Core Components**:
 - Instruction: clear directive of what model should do.
 - Context: Background/additional info to understand the task
 - Input: Specify query/data model need to process
 - O/P Type/format: Desired structure or style or tone of response

**Prompt Engineering Techniques**:
   - Zero-shot Prompting
   - Few-shot Prompting
   - Prompt Chaining
   - Chain of Thoughts

**Why do we need to design better prompts?**

  - High Quality, Coherent & relevant response
  - Well-Designed Prompts helps prevent Bias, hallucination & data leakage.
  - Predictable results
  - Tailored output for specific taks.
